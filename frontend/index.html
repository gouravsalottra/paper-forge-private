How it works

One researcher journey, not a maze of tools

Thrivarc keeps the experience centered on the research question while the system absorbs the process complexity underneath.

1. Clarify the question Thrivarc turns a raw curiosity into a study worth running, including exploratory work when a hypothesis is not ready yet.
2. Choose or stage the evidence Upload your own dataset, use public market data, or stage an external source for preview.
3. Approve the research blueprint Before anything runs, you see the method logic, cadence logic, evidence route, validation burden, and planned stages.
4. Let the engine execute and self-challenge Data collection, methodology checks, statistical analysis, reviewer simulation, and revision loops happen in sequence.
5. Read findings, criticism, and paper-ready outputs The result arrives with reviewer pressure, follow-up recommendations, and writing support built on verified outputs.

FAQ

Questions researchers actually ask

Thrivarc is designed for real finance research, so the product has to answer the operational questions, not just the marketing ones.

Can I start without a hypothesis?

Yes. Exploratory research is a first-class path. Thrivarc helps turn a broad topic into a sharper study before you commit to a claim.

Can I use my own data?

Yes. Upload directly now, or stage an external extract and review the schema before the run begins. Public connectors can still supply benchmarks and controls.

Will I only get positive findings?

No. Null findings are valid outcomes. Thrivarc is built to tell you the truth, not to flatter the hypothesis.

Is this only for finance?

Today the system is focused on empirical finance and adjacent market research. The product language, data connectors, and testing flow are tuned for that domain.
